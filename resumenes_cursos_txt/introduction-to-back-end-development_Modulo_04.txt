TITLE: About The Ungraded Lab Improve Your Bio Page With Bootstrap Instructions

In the next item, you will complete an Ungraded Lab where you will update the biographical page you created in Week 2 - Introduction to HTML5 and CSS to use Bootstrap.

The expected outcome is a two-column biographical page with your name and a photo in the left column and your favorite music artists and films in the right column.

Note:

Your profile image may look slightly different from the example image depending on the provided photo.jpg file and your browser preview size. Focus on whether the image is responsive and whether the page layout matches the expected structure. On larger screens, the bio section and profile image should appear on the left, while the lists and profile button should appear on the right. On smaller screens, these sections should stack vertically.

Proceed to the next item to begin the Ungraded Lab.

TITLE: Exemplar Instructions

By updating your biographical page to use Bootstrap, your new page should be similar to the image below.

Your HTML file structure and content should be similar to the snippet below. Note where the Bootstrap CSS classes were used in the different HTML elements.

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
<title>My Bio Page</title>
<link href="bootstrap.min.css" rel="stylesheet">
</head>
<body>
<div class="container">
<div class="row">
<div id="bio" class="col-12 col-lg-6 text-center">
<h1>Jane</h1>
<img src="photo.jpg" class="img-fluid" />
</div>
<div id="more" class="col-12 col-md-6">
<h2>Favorite Music Artists</h2>
<ul>
<li>Metallica</li>
<li>Bob Marley</li>
<li>Madonna</li>
<li>The Beatles</li>
<li>Pink Floyd</li>
</ul>
<h2>Favorite Films</h2>
<ol>
<li>Pulp Fiction</li>
<li>The Godfather</li>
<li>The Lord of the Rings</li>
<li>Iron Man</li>
<li>Inception</li>
</ol>
<a href="https://www.meta.com/user/123" class="btn btn-primary">My Meta Profile</a>
</div>
</div>
</div>
<script src="bootstrap.bundle.min.js"></script>
</body>
</html>

Note: This example assumes you have the Bootstrap CSS and JS files downloaded to your local folder. If you are coding this from scratch, you will need to replace the href and src links in the head and body with the official Bootstrap CDN links. Additionally, remember that if your browser preview window is small, the columns will stack vertically. You must maximize your browser window to see the side-by-side layout!

As a developer, Bootstrap is one of the many libraries in your toolkit to help build web applications. Bootstrap has many utilities and components. We encourage you to read their documentation and experiment further with styling your webpage.
